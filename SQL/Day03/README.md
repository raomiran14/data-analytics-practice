# Day 03 - SQL HAVING Clause

## Topics Covered

- GROUP BY
- HAVING
- COUNT()
- SUM()
- AVG()
- ORDER BY
- Filtering grouped results

## Dataset

Used the sales dataset from Day 01.

The dataset contains:

- Order information
- Customer names
- Product categories
- Quantity
- Price
- Region

## Questions Solved

1. Find categories that sold more than 10 products.
2. Find regions with more than 3 orders.
3. Find categories with an average price greater than 800.
4. Find regions with total revenue greater than 5,000.
5. Find categories with at least 5 orders.
6. Find regions where average price is less than 900.
7. Find categories where total quantity is between 8 and 15.
8. Find regions with more than 2 orders and average price greater than 700.
9. Find categories with total revenue greater than 9,000.
10. Display regions with revenue greater than 5,000 in descending order.

## Key Learning

Learned that WHERE filters individual rows,
while HAVING filters grouped results.

Practiced using aggregate functions with HAVING
to filter categories and regions.
